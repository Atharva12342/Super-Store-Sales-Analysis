# Super Store Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Super Store Sales Analysis is a data analysis project that analyzes sales data from a super store to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and profit trends.

The project uses data analysis and visualization techniques to identify important business insights and support better decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit.
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Analyze sales based on regions and segments.
* Find profitable and low-performing products.
* Visualize important sales trends and patterns.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Import the required libraries.
2. Load the Super Store dataset.
3. Understand the structure of the data.
4. Clean and preprocess the dataset.
5. Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
6. Analyze sales, profit, products, and customers.
7. Create charts and visualizations.
8. Generate useful business insights.

## 📊 Key Analysis

The project analyzes:

* **Sales:** Overall sales and sales trends.
* **Profit:** Profitability of products and categories.
* **Products:** Best and worst-performing products.
* **Categories:** Sales and profit by product category.
* **Regions:** Regional sales and profit comparison.
* **Customers:** Customer segments and purchasing behavior.
* **Time:** Monthly and yearly sales trends.

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes visualizations such as:

* Sales by Category
* Profit by Category
* Sales by Region
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Top 10 Products by Profit
* Customer Segment Analysis
